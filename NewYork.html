New York City🗽

Walkability in New York City

New York City is widely regarded as one of the most walkable cities in the United States. The city's dense urban environment, extensive public transportation system, and pedestrian-friendly infrastructure make it easy for residents and visitors to get around on foot.

According to Walk Score, a website that measures walkability, New York City has an overall Walk Score of 88 out of 100, which is considered "very walkable." This means that most errands can be accomplished on foot, and there are plenty of amenities within walking distance.

Boston☘️

Walkability in Boston

Boston is another city that is known for its walkability. The city's compact layout, historic neighborhoods, and pedestrian-friendly streets make it easy to get around on foot.

According to Walk Score, Boston has an overall Walk Score of 83 out of 100, which is considered "very walkable." This means that most errands can be accomplished on foot, and there are plenty of amenities within walking distance.

Chicago🐻

Walkability in Chicago

Chicago is a city that has made significant efforts to improve its walkability in recent years. The city's downtown area, known as the Loop, is particularly pedestrian-friendly, with wide sidewalks, crosswalks, and public spaces.

According to Walk Score, Chicago has an overall Walk Score of 77 out of 100, which is considered "very walkable." This means that most errands can be accomplished on foot, and there are plenty of amenities within walking distance.

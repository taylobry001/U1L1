Beyond the Car: Designing Better US Neighborhoods

In order to design more walkable and bikeable cities, we need to prioritize pedestrians and cyclists over cars. This means creating more walkable and bikeable streets, with dedicated lanes for each mode of transportation. We also need to invest in public transportation, making it more accessible and affordable for everyone. Finally, we need to rethink the way we use our public spaces, creating more parks and green spaces that encourage people to spend time outside and connect with their communities.

By prioritizing pedestrians and cyclists over cars, we can create healthier, more sustainable, and more equitable communities. We can also reduce traffic congestion, air pollution, and greenhouse gas emissions, while improving public health and social interaction. Ultimately, designing better US neighborhoods is about creating a better quality of life for everyone.
